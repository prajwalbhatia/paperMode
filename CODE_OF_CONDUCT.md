# PaperMode community code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to contribute, ask questions and learn, regardless of background, identity, disability or experience. Treat people with respect, discuss the work rather than attacking the person, and give feedback that someone can act on.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized abuse, repeated unwanted contact, spam and sharing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable. Good-faith disagreement and constructive criticism are welcome.

These expectations apply to PaperMode issues, pull requests and other project spaces, and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and moderation

For a project moderation request about public content, mention [@prajwalbhatia](https://github.com/prajwalbhatia) on the relevant thread when safe. Link to the content rather than repeating harmful material or disclosing private information.

For a private abuse or harassment report, use GitHub's **Report content** option where available or [Report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse). Those reports are handled by GitHub; PaperMode does not currently operate a separate confidential conduct inbox. Do not post sensitive incident details publicly. Security vulnerabilities have their own private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, request a change in behavior, limit participation, or block repeated or serious abuse. The response should reflect the severity and pattern of behavior. When appropriate, maintainers will explain moderation decisions without exposing reporters' private information. Retaliation against someone raising a good-faith concern is not acceptable.
