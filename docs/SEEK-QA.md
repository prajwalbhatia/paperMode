Windowed play/pause worked first; its seek initially reported unavailable because Firefox left focus on the video web area after the play action. The generic Firefox seek path now accepts that focused web area only when its URL matches the bound document and the timed slider is valid.

QA review follow-up: A browser-window race could direct an action to another window in the same process, so the helper now rechecks the focused AX window and verifies that the bound document still belongs to it immediately before pressing or posting input. Firefox's generic seek no longer treats focus anywhere on the page's web area as player focus. It accepts focus within the bound player or explicitly focuses the bound timeline and verifies the new focus before sending an arrow. Generic seek now checks that the displayed elapsed time agrees with seconds, percentage, or millisecond slider values, allowing small rounding differences. Native tests cover conflicting time values; 65 JavaScript tests and package verification passed. In the rebuilt local app, the owner confirmed InterviewReady playback, both seeks, and unlock after the guard changes; a windowed Vimeo retest confirmed seeking still moved the video after play/pause. The owner also passed one final playback, both-seek, and unlock regression in the rebuilt app with the document-parent check. The window-switch race and outside-player focus refusal have not been physically induced.

Further QA review found that focus on any descendant of a player group could send a plain Firefox arrow to its volume slider. The helper now accepts focus only on the bound timeline or exact player surface; otherwise it tries to focus the timeline and verifies the result before posting an arrow. The owner then confirmed Firefox YouTube playback and both seek directions still worked after play/pause.
